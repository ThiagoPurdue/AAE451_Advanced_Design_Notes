These notes are written to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the standard adopted under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act for the web content of public universities.

## What the site does

- Every figure has alt text that describes what it shows, and a visible caption. Diagrams are SVG files with real text, so they stay sharp when zoomed.
- Tables are real HTML tables with header rows and captions, not images of tables.
- Color is never the only way information is given. Where the lecture slide used color, for example in the compliance matrix, the notes add a text column.
- Text and figure colors meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against their background.
- Pages use headings in order, so a screen reader can move through them by section.
- Links name their destination.
- The site can be used with a keyboard alone. It follows the reader's light or dark mode setting, and text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content.
- Each page is checked with the axe accessibility engine before it is published.

## Known limitations

- Twelve figures are photographs or images of third-party material: the MRJ90 photograph and two illustrations in Lecture 1, and the nine aircraft photographs in Lecture 3, some reused in Lectures 4 and 5. Two of them contain text: the Wisk Aero urban air mobility illustration and the 2022 student mission statement slide. Their content is given in the alt text and in the text around them.
- The YouTube videos the lecture recommends are hosted by YouTube. Their captions are provided by YouTube, not by this site.
- Linked external documents, such as the RFP and the military standards, are outside the control of this site.

## Report a barrier

If any part of these notes is hard to use with your browser, screen reader, or other assistive technology, contact the instructor through the course Brightspace page. Name the page and describe the problem. You will get the content in a form you can use. Students can also request accommodations through the Purdue Disability Resource Center.
